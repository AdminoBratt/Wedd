# Landing Page Brief: Wedding & Corporate Event Planner

## Business
- **Name:** *Sigge Bratt Event* (placeholder; swap for the real name)
- **Location:** Stockholm (placeholder). **Local clients only**: no destination events.
- **Focus:** Two lines, **weddings** and **corporate events**.

## Target customers
- **Couples** planning a wedding, across **mixed budget levels** (not only luxury).
- **Corporate:** small and local businesses (holiday parties, client evenings, anniversaries) and mid-to-large companies (conferences, galas, product launches, award nights).

## Services
- **Weddings:** Full planning only, from the first meeting through the wedding day.
- **Corporate:** End-to-end event planning: concept, venue, vendors, logistics, and running the event.
- **Pricing:** Not shown anywhere on the page. Every project is quoted after a conversation. The copy should still say that different budget levels are welcome.

## Tone & visual direction
- **Elegant & refined:** calm, polished, understated luxury.
- Serif display headlines, a restrained palette, generous white space, and editorial photography.
- It must feel credible to corporate buyers too: refined, not frilly.

## Language
- All page copy in **Swedish**.

## Primary goal / CTA
- **Get visitors to call or email directly.** The page has no forms.
- Phone and email stay prominent: in the hero, in the contact section, and reachable from the header on every scroll position (for example a sticky "Ring / Mejla" button).
- Use `tel:` and `mailto:` links with placeholder number and address.

## Sections (in order)
1. **Header:** logo/name, anchor nav, call/email button.
2. **Hero:** headline, a one-line positioning statement (weddings + corporate events in Stockholm), phone and email CTAs, a full-bleed photo.
3. **Tjänster (Services):** two side-by-side cards, Bröllop (full planning) and Företagsevent (end-to-end).
4. **Så går det till (Process):** 3–4 steps: first call → proposal & quote → planning → the day.
5. **Portfolio / galleri:** a grid of wedding and corporate event photos.
6. **Om mig / oss (About):** the planner's story, a photo, values.
7. **FAQ:** lead time, budget levels, vendors, what's included, corporate vs wedding.
8. **Kontakt:** large phone and email with a short reassuring line. This is the closing CTA.
9. **Footer:** name, contact, social links (placeholders).

Not included: testimonials, partner logos, Instagram feed, pricing.

## Content
- **Everything is placeholder** for now: Swedish sample copy and licensed stand-in photos (for example Unsplash), to be replaced with real content later.
- Keep placeholder text and images easy to find and swap (centralized content/data file).

## Tech
- **Next.js / React.**
- One page, responsive and mobile-first (most visitors will tap the phone number on mobile), accessible, fast.
